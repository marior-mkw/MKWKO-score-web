## Data displayed publicly

A board's public URL may display:

- board and tournament names;
- team tags, names, and colors;
- race positions and team points;
- accumulated totals, race progress, scoring configuration, and update time.

Do not enter personal information or secrets in these fields.

## Data not included in the public projection

The public page is designed not to receive or display board-owner IDs, editor-role IDs, scorekeeper IDs, Discord interaction IDs, credentials, or internal generation metadata.

## Infrastructure data

GitHub Pages, Firebase, Google Cloud, Discord, and the viewer's browser may process ordinary service logs, network metadata, or cookies according to their own policies. This static page does not intentionally set an application cookie or send browser credentials with Firebase requests.

## Access and deletion

Authorized Discord users can remove a board with `/mk delete`. Privacy and deletion requests may be sent to **[PRIVACY CONTACT]**. The operator should publish a retention period for inactive boards and explain any backup retention separately.

## Security

The application uses HTTPS, restrictive browser policies, bounded responses, sanitized public data, and read-only Firebase access for browsers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
